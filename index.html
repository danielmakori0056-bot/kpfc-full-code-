NEED TO KNOW?

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions about shopping with KPFC.

Still have a question? Contact us
Where is KPFC Builders Hardware located?

We are in Kikuyu Town, opposite Kikuyu Police Station along the Nairobi–Nakuru Highway, Kenya.

How can I confirm product prices and availability?

Product prices may change. Send us an enquiry on WhatsApp or call our team to confirm current prices and stock before ordering.

Can I order more than one product?

Yes. Add products to your enquiry cart and send the list to our team through WhatsApp to discuss your order.

Does KPFC offer delivery?

Contact our team with your location and order details to confirm available delivery arrangements and any applicable charges.
